# 📻 Walkie Friends - Talk with Friends

A simple, fast, and confidential Walkie-Talkie app designed for talking with your friends in real-time.

---

## ✨ Features for You & Your Friends

- 🎙️ **Giant Push-To-Talk Button**: Press and hold to talk (or hold the **Spacebar** on your keyboard). Release to listen.
- 🔒 **Confidential Channels (PIN Protection)**: Lock your channel with a 4-digit PIN or secret word. Voice is encrypted end-to-end so nobody else can listen in.
- 📡 **Multiple Channels & Frequencies**: Tap `CH 1`, `CH 2`, `CH 3`, or create any custom channel (e.g. `Gaming`, `Secret Squad`, `Room 42`).
- 👥 **Live Friends In Channel**: See who is in your channel in real-time. When a friend speaks, their avatar pulses green!
- 🎧 **Hands-Free Mode**: Tap *Hands-Free: ON* to chat continuously while gaming or doing other tasks.
- 🔗 **One-Click Invite Link**: Tap **Invite** to copy a link (e.g. `http://localhost:3000/?channel=2&pin=1234`). Send it to your friends on WhatsApp or Discord—when they click it, they join your room immediately!
- 📱 **Works on Phones & Computers**: Open the app on your phone, laptop, or desktop over the same Wi-Fi.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

```bash
npm start
```

1. **On your computer**: Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)
2. **On your phone**: Open [https://<YOUR-IP>:3443](https://<YOUR-IP>:3443) or scan the QR code from the **Invite** button.
3. Choose a channel, enter a PIN if you want it private, and start talking!
